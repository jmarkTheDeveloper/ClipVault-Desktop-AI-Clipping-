# Customer Support & Client Success SOP

## Standard Operating Procedure: SOP-OPS-001
**Objective**: Deliver world-class, fast (<4 hour SLA) customer support to ClipVault license holders and trial users.

---

## 1. Primary Support Channels
- **Official Help Email**: `support@clipvault.app`
- **Merchant of Record Order Portal**: Lemon Squeezy Customer Invoices & Subscriptions
- **Target Response Time**: Within 4 business hours (Maximum 24 hours).

---

## 2. Common Customer Inquiry Playbooks

### Inquiry 1: "Where do I find my license key?"
* **Response Template**:
  > Hi [First Name],  
  >  
  > Thanks for supporting ClipVault! Your commercial license key was sent directly to your email receipt from Lemon Squeezy with the subject line *"Your ClipVault Order #..."*.  
  >  
  > You can also copy your key at any time by opening your Lemon Squeezy customer portal: [https://app.lemonsqueezy.com/my-orders](https://app.lemonsqueezy.com/my-orders).  
  >  
  > Once you have your key:  
  > 1. Launch ClipVault on your desktop.  
  > 2. Paste your key into the License Activation dialog.  
  > 3. Click **Activate License**—your full Pro features will unlock instantly!  
  >  
  > Let us know if you need any assistance getting started!

---

### Inquiry 2: "I'm switching to a new PC. How do I transfer my license?"
* **Response Template**:
  > Hi [First Name],  
  >  
  > Absolutely! ClipVault licenses can easily be transferred between machines:  
  > 1. On your old PC, open **Settings** $\rightarrow$ click **Deactivate License** (or simply uninstall the app).  
  > 2. Download ClipVault on your new PC.  
  > 3. Enter your original license key on the new computer.  
  >  
  > If your old machine crashed and you cannot deactivate it manually, let us know and we will reset your machine binding in our licensing database for you right away!

---

### Inquiry 3: "How do I request a refund under the 14-day guarantee?"

  >

  >
