# The ClipVault \$3,000–\$5,000/Month Retainer Agency Playbook

## 1. Executive Concept
Instead of competing in public bounty pools or waiting for YouTube Shorts AdSense approval, you can use ClipVault as a "Done-For-You" B2B clipping agency:
- **The Value Proposition**: Deliver 30 high-retention, professionally framed and captioned vertical videos every month to busy business owners, podcasters, or mid-sized creators.
- **The Price Point**: **\$500 to \$1,000 per month** per client.
- **Your Time Investment**: Under **1 hour of actual software work per client per month** using ClipVault.

## 3. The 4-Step Agency Execution Process

### Step 1: Free Value Hook (The "No-Brainer" Pitch)
1. Pick a creator with 10k–100k subscribers who has 1-hour long podcast episodes on YouTube.
2. Open ClipVault in Consumer Mode (`npm run consumer`).
3. Paste their latest episode URL and generate 3 viral 9:16 vertical clips (with animated captions and face tracking).
4. Send them a short, non-pushy message (see `COLD_OUTREACH_TEMPLATES.md`):
   > *"Hey [Name], loved your recent episode on [Topic]! I turned your best moment at 14:20 into a ready-to-post vertical Short with dynamic captions and auto-reframing. Here’s the video file (free, no catch). If you want 30 of these delivered every month for \$500 so you never have to think about Shorts, let me know!"*

### Step 2: The Onboarding Call (15 Minutes)
- Align on their preferred caption style (e.g. `Hormozi / Bold Yellow` or `Beast Mode`).
- Confirm their target posting platforms (TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, LinkedIn).
- Send the Client Service Agreement (see `Legal_and_Contracts/04_CLIENT_SERVICE_AGREEMENT.md`).
- Collect payment upfront via Stripe or Lemon Squeezy invoice.

### Step 3: Production Day (45 Minutes)
- Once or twice a month, open ClipVault.
- Batch process their episodes into 30 clips.
- Upload finished clips to a shared Google Drive or Dropbox folder organized by week (`Week 1`, `Week 2`, `Week 3`, `Week 4`).
- Include the AI-generated catchy headlines and descriptions from ClipVault's `*.metadata.txt` files for instant copy-pasting.

### Step 4: Retainer Scaling & Expansion
- **Client 1**: \$500/month (1 hr work)
- **Client 3**: \$1,500/month (3 hrs work)
- **Client 6**: **\$3,000/month** (6 hrs work)
- **Client 10**: **\$5,000/month** (10 hrs work)
- At 10 clients, you can hire a junior assistant to run ClipVault and review clips while you focus purely on client acquisition.
