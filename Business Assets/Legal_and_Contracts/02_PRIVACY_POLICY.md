# ClipVault Privacy Policy (Local-First Compliance)

**Last Updated**: October 28, 2026  
**Entity**: ClipVault Studio LLC  
**Commitment**: Local-First Privacy, Zero Data Selling, GDPR & CCPA Compliant  

---

## 1. Our Privacy Philosophy: Local-First Processing
Unlike conventional cloud video editing SaaS platforms that require uploading all your video and audio files to third-party cloud servers, **ClipVault processes video locally on your personal machine**.
- Your raw video files are **never uploaded to our servers**.
- Your face tracking coordinates and camera frames stay on your local GPU/RAM.
- Your rendered output clips are saved directly to your local hard drive.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Information You Provide Directly
- **Billing & Account Details**: When purchasing a license via Lemon Squeezy, we collect your email address, name, and license transaction ID. We never see or store full credit card numbers.
- **License Activation Key**: Required to cryptographically verify software entitlement on your device.

### B. Minimal Telemetry & Diagnostics (Opt-Out Capable)
- **Application Startup Status**: Anonymous ping verifying whether the local engine started successfully.
- **Crash Diagnostics**: Non-identifiable error logs (e.g. GPU encoder failure notices) used exclusively to resolve bugs.

---

## 3. How We Use Information
We use collected data solely to:
1. Verify license status and grant software access.
2. Deliver software updates, critical bug patches, and security advisories.
3. Comply with global taxation obligations (EU VAT, US State Sales Tax) via our Merchant of Record.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services Used
- **Lemon Squeezy**: Merchant of Record and payment gateway.
- **Google Gemini API / Anthropic Claude API** *(Optional)*: If you provide your own API keys for AI transcript curation, text snippets (subtitles only, never video frames) are transmitted directly to the chosen AI provider under their standard enterprise API data protection policies.

---

## 5. Your Data Rights (GDPR & CCPA)
Under applicable privacy laws, you have the right to:
- Access, correct, or delete any personal account data stored in our licensing database.
- Opt out of diagnostic communications at any time.
- Inquire about data processing practices by contacting `privacy@clipvault.app`.
