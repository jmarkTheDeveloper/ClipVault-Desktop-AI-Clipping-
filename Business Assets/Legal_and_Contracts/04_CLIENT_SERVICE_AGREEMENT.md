## 1. Scope of Services
Agency agrees to provide Client with professional short-form video clipping and reformatting services, consisting of:
- **Deliverables**: Thirty (30) edited 9:16 vertical video clips per month derived from Client’s long-form video/podcast episodes.
- **Editing Specifications**: Active speaker face tracking & auto-reframe, animated word-level typography/captions, color grading, and optional background audio.
- **Supporting Assets**: Catchy social media headlines and optimized description copy for every clip.
- **Turnaround Time**: Delivered in weekly batches (7–8 clips every 7 calendar days).

---

## 2. Compensation & Retainer Terms
- **Monthly Fee**: Client agrees to pay Agency a fixed recurring retainer fee of **\$____________ USD per month**.
- **Payment Schedule**: Invoiced upfront on the 1st of every service period via Stripe / Bank Transfer / Credit Card.
- **Late Payments**: Deliverables for the subsequent period will pause until outstanding invoices are settled.

---

## 3. Client Responsibilities & Asset Access
Client shall provide Agency with timely access to raw long-form audio/video files or public YouTube/Vimeo URLs at least 48 hours prior to scheduled delivery deadlines.

---

## 4. Ownership of Work Product
Upon full payment of the monthly retainer, **Client holds 100% full commercial copyright and ownership of all delivered vertical video clips**. Agency retains the non-exclusive right to display sample clips in Agency portfolio and case studies.

---

## 5. Term & Cancellation
- **Initial Term**: This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for a minimum period of thirty (30) days.
- **Renewal & Cancellation**: Renews automatically month-to-month. Either party may terminate with at least **fourteen (14) days written notice** prior to the next billing cycle.
