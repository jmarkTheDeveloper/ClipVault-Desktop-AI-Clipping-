# B2B Cold Outreach Templates for \$500–\$1,000/Mo Retainers

## Strategy Overview: "Give Value First"
Never pitch cold without showing proof. Always generate **one finished, captioned, face-tracked clip of the prospect’s own content** using ClipVault first, attach it, and let the quality speak for itself.

---

## Template 1: The "Free Sample" DM (Instagram / Twitter / X)
*Use this for mid-tier podcasters (10k–100k subscribers) or personal brand creators.*

> **Subject / Opening Line**: Quick clip I made from your latest episode with [Guest Name]!  
>  
> Hey [First Name],  
>  
> Loved your point at 18:40 in your recent podcast about [specific topic]. I know how much time cutting Shorts takes, so I threw together a ready-to-post 9:16 vertical clip with kinetic captions and active face reframing for you:  
>  
> 🔗 [Link to finished sample video file / Google Drive]  
>  
> Feel free to post it on your Shorts/TikTok—it’s 100% yours, zero catch.  
>  
> If you like the style and want **30 of these delivered every month for \$500** so you never have to think about short-form editing again, let me know and I can set it up this week!  
>  
> Best,  
> [Your Name]

## Template 2: The B2B / Business Owner Pitch (LinkedIn / Email)
*Use this for real estate brokers, law firm partners, financial advisors, and agency owners who publish webinars or YouTube videos.*

> **Subject**: Idea for [Company Name]'s short-form video reach  
>  
> Hi [First Name],  
>  
> I came across your recent presentation on [Topic] and noticed you have great long-form insights, but haven’t been distributing them as vertical Shorts on LinkedIn/Instagram.  
>  
> To show you what's possible, I took a 45-second highlight from your video and formatted it into a vertical studio short with auto-reframing and dynamic captions:  
>  
> 🔗 [Link to preview clip]  
>  
> We help busy executives turn their existing videos and podcasts into **30 ready-to-publish short-form videos every month** on a flat **\$750/month retainer**, handling all editing, hook selection, and captions.  
>  
> Would you be open to a quick 10-minute chat this Thursday to see if this makes sense for [Company Name]?  
>  
> Warm regards,  
> [Your Name]  
> [Your Title / Agency Name]
