# ClipVault Brand Guidelines & Positioning

## 1. Brand Identity & Positioning Statement
> **"ClipVault is the high-performance local AI video studio that gives creators unlimited, private, and instant 9:16 viral video clipping—powered by your machine's own silicon, with zero cloud credit limits."**

---

## 2. Core Messaging Pillars
1. **Unlimited Freedom**: No artificial credit countdowns. Clip as much content as your hardware can render.
2. **Local-First Privacy**: Your media stays on your SSD. Safe for NDA content and confidential client footage.
3. **Hardware Acceleration**: Built for real creator hardware—Intel QuickSync, NVIDIA NVENC, and AMD AMF silicon.
4. **Cinematic Precision**: Intelligent virtual camera reframing that never jerks or jitters.

---

## 3. Brand Tone of Voice
- **Authoritative & Professional**: We sound like high-end desktop creative tools (DaVinci Resolve, Adobe, Linear).
- **No Gimmicks**: We avoid cartoonish hype, fake countdown timers, or spammy marketing clichés.
- **Empowering**: We treat the user as a serious creator, agency operator, or media professional.

---

## 4. Visual Color Palette

| Name | Hex Code | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Studio Dark Background** | `#09090b` / `#121418` | Primary desktop app backdrop & modal surfaces |
| **Zinc Border & Accents** | `#27272a` (zinc-800) | Subtle borders, divider seams, structural lines |
| **Emerald Active Accent** | `#10b981` (emerald-500) | Success indicators, primary action buttons, active progress tracks |
| **Amber Warning Accent** | `#f59e0b` (amber-500) | Caution notices, task exit confirmation badges |
| **Pure White** | `#ffffff` | Primary text headlines & high-contrast titles |
| **Muted Slate Text** | `#a1a1aa` (zinc-400) | Secondary body copy, timestamps, explanatory labels |

---

## 5. Logo Usage & Safe Zones
- The official ClipVault icon uses a modern rounded-square squircle geometry (`rounded-2xl` or `14px`).
- Always maintain clear margins equal to at least 25% of the logo's height on all sides.
- Never distort, stretch, rotate, or apply outer neon halos to the official logo mark.
